## 1 Who we are and how to contact us

Westmere Careers is a service operated by **Louis Jacques Pierre Sibul**, a sole trader trading as **Westmere Systems**, **ABN 16 319 214 903** (Westmere, we, us or our).

Our Privacy Officer is responsible for privacy enquiries, requests and complaints. It also explains overseas processing, your choices and how to raise a concern.

We handle personal information in accordance with applicable Australian privacy laws. These include the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and Australian Privacy Principles (APPs) where applicable, and the Health Records Act 2001 (Vic) and its Health Privacy Principles where we handle health information within its scope. Any Victorian public sector privacy obligations apply to us only to the extent the law or a relevant government services arrangement makes them applicable. Nothing in this policy excludes a legal right or obligation.

This policy explains our practices; reading it or using the website does not, by itself, give consent to marketing, sensitive-information collection or optional AI processing. We obtain any consent required for the particular activity separately.

## 2 The information we collect

Personal information includes information or an opinion about an identified or reasonably identifiable person, whether accurate or not. Depending on the features you use, we collect the following kinds of information.

**Accounts and profiles.** Your email address, account identifier, optional full name, saved profile and CV text, account creation and update times, authentication and session records, and account or subscription status. Our current sign-in flow uses an emailed link rather than a Westmere password.

**Career and application information.** The information you put in your CV, cover letter, application answers or career goals. This may include contact details, education, qualifications, employment history, skills, achievements, work preferences and referee details. We do not require every one of these details; what we receive depends on what you choose to include. For a direct application, we also collect the job and employer identifiers, a copy of the submitted material, role details at submission, submission time, application stage and withdrawal time.

**Application activity and employer updates.** Recorded receipt and first intentional opening events, the account associated with an action, event times, application decisions and messages entered by authorised hiring team members. Messages and decisions may contain personal information about candidates and employer representatives.

**Employer accounts and listings.** Employer organisation details, representative account identifiers, organisation membership and permission levels, job advertisements, publication status, salary and role requirements, and information provided in business verification or support correspondence. Company information can be personal information where it identifies a sole trader or individual representative. A listing or organisation profile is intended for public display; do not include private candidate information in it.

**Experience reports.** Your account and relevant application or job reference, employer, application date, whether a response was received, response date, the source of the report, confirmation time and moderation status. These reports are separate from activity recorded by Westmere.

**Careers+ and payments.** Your email address and account reference, Stripe customer, subscription and checkout references, price and subscription status, billing-period dates, cancellation status and limited payment-event and reconciliation records. Stripe separately collects payment details and any billing, verification and fraud-prevention information required for its services.

**Preparation tools.** CV text, task selection, optional goals and supplied role title or description used for a requested comparison or AI review, and the resulting suggestions. We also record account-linked AI allowance records, including request mode, status, billing period and relevant times. Those allowance records do not contain your CV or the generated text.

**Website and security information.** Our hosting, authentication and delivery providers may process IP addresses, browser and device information, request URLs, search parameters, timestamps, errors, authentication events and security or delivery logs. Search filters can reveal career interests. Location filters contain the location you enter; the site does not request precise device geolocation, camera or microphone access.

**Communications.** The contact details and content you provide when you communicate with us, associated correspondence and, if optional marketing is introduced and you subscribe, your consent and unsubscribe records. Avoid putting unnecessary sensitive information in ordinary email.

## 3 How information reaches us and your choice to provide it

We usually collect information directly from you when you enter information, sign in, save a profile, request a tool, apply, report an experience or contact us. We also receive information from hiring teams about your application, from Stripe about subscriptions and payment events, and from service providers about authentication, email delivery, operation and security. Information you submit may include information about another person, such as a referee.

We collect information by lawful and fair means and limit collection to what is reasonably necessary for our services or other lawful activities. If we receive information indirectly, we take reasonable steps to give any collection notice required by law, subject to lawful exceptions. If we receive unsolicited personal information that we could not lawfully have collected, we destroy or de-identify it where lawful and reasonable.

You can browse public roles and employer pages without an account, although ordinary network and security processing still occurs. You can generally make a broad enquiry anonymously or under a pseudonym where practicable. We need an email address to establish an account, application material to submit an application, and necessary payment information through Stripe to provide a paid subscription. Without the relevant information, we may be unable to provide that feature or respond to an individual account request.

You do not need to save a CV in your profile to browse roles. Optional AI assistance and CV comparison can be declined. An account, application, subscription or support enquiry does not automatically subscribe you to marketing.

The current forms do not require a tax file number, Medicare number, passport, driver's licence, bank account details, medical certificate or criminal-history document. Do not include these in a CV or general enquiry. If a later lawful process needs identity or work-rights verification, we will explain the specific requirements and collection arrangements first. We do not adopt a government identifier as your Westmere account identifier.

If you include another person's details, tell them why you are providing those details and obtain their permission where appropriate. Do not submit someone else's CV or confidential information without authority. Westmere does not automatically contact referees because they are listed in a CV.

## 4 Sensitive information and health information

Sensitive information can include health or disability information, racial or ethnic origin, religious or political beliefs, sexual orientation, trade-union or professional-association membership, criminal records and certain biometric information. Some of this information can appear in a CV or free-text application even though we do not ask for it.

Please leave out sensitive information unless it is necessary for the particular purpose. Where we need to collect sensitive information, we seek an informed and specific consent and collect only what is reasonably necessary, unless an applicable legal exception permits or requires collection. Simply accepting this policy is not that consent. We handle health information under applicable Victorian health privacy requirements as well as any applicable federal requirements.

We do not use sensitive information for marketing. Our CV assistance is instructed not to infer protected characteristics or assess suitability using them. That instruction does not guarantee that AI output will be accurate or free of bias. Remove sensitive details and other people's personal information before an AI request unless there is a clear, lawful need to include them.

If you have shared sensitive information by mistake or need an accessible way to use our services, contact our Privacy Officer. We will assess how to minimise, correct, restrict or remove the information consistently with applicable law.

## 5 What we use information for

We use personal information to provide the features you request: establish and secure accounts; store profiles; find and display roles; process applications and withdrawals; give authorised hiring teams access to submitted material; display application activity and decisions; provide requested preparation tools; and manage subscription access and usage allowances.

We also use relevant information to answer enquiries and privacy requests, investigate complaints and suspected misuse, maintain and troubleshoot the service, reconcile billing, keep necessary financial and operational records, enforce lawful terms, and comply with legal obligations or valid legal processes. Where information is used to improve the service, we minimise it and use de-identified or aggregated information where practicable.

We do not sell candidate CVs, contact lists or application histories. A privately saved CV is not a publicly searchable candidate profile, and employers cannot browse the private CV database. We do not use your CV or application content to train our own general-purpose AI model or authorise third-party model training on that content.

We use or disclose information for the purpose of collection, another purpose you consent to, or another purpose permitted or required by law. For a secondary use without consent, any applicable reasonable-expectation and related-purpose requirements must be met; sensitive information attracts the stricter directly related purpose requirement. We do not treat a general reference to improving services as permission for an unrelated use.

## 6 Your CV and applications

Text pasted into an editor, or read from an imported plain-text file, starts in the current page's memory. Merely importing a .txt file does not upload that file to a document store or send its text to OpenAI. The current app does not save CV text in localStorage or sessionStorage. Browser features, extensions, spellcheck or your device may handle text under their own settings.

When you deliberately save a signed-in profile, its name and CV text are stored privately in our Supabase database. When you submit an application on Westmere, we create a separate submission snapshot containing the CV text, cover letter and answers supplied for that application, together with relevant role details and time. Editing your profile later does not change that snapshot. For an error in a submitted application, contact us about correction or annotation rather than assuming an edit to your profile has corrected it.

Submitting a direct application shares its contents with the organisation identified for that role and its authorised hiring team. If the advertiser is a recruitment agency, the initial recipient is that agency. Check the advertiser's privacy notice and ask it about any proposed onward disclosure to an end employer. We do not automatically circulate your application to unrelated employers.

Withdrawal stops further hiring-team access to the application content through the current Westmere workspace. A limited record of the application and withdrawal can remain. Withdrawal does not automatically erase our records or recall copies already seen, copied or otherwise retained by the recipient. Ask us separately about deletion and contact the recipient about its own records.

If a role sends you to an external application website, that operator collects information under its own privacy arrangements. Westmere does not receive or track the external submission or its opening merely because you followed a link. Your browser will disclose ordinary connection information to that website.

## 7 Application activity and experience reports

For applications submitted on Westmere, a recorded opening means an authorised hiring team member intentionally opened the detail view through the relevant feature. Loading an applicant list does not create that opening event. An opening is not proof of a full read, shortlisting or a job offer. Employer-entered decisions and messages can appear in the candidate's timeline. A message recorded in the app is not evidence that an email was sent, delivered or read.

If you are an employer representative, your account identifier is associated with relevant application actions for authorisation and accountability. The candidate timeline shows the event, its time and any message; the current timeline does not display the actor identifier. We may examine activity records when investigating access, accuracy or misuse.

Application-experience reports are recorded privately for review and are not automatically published. Live public response aggregation, moderation and appeals are not operating in the current development release; displayed sample figures are fictional. If a public reporting service is introduced, we will explain it before using reports for that purpose, apply moderation and safeguards against identification, and provide correction and complaint arrangements. We will not publish a candidate's CV, contact details or identifiable report as a public employer review without an appropriate lawful basis.

Removing names alone does not make information anonymous. We consider whether small samples, dates, role details or other available information could identify someone before treating information as de-identified or publishing aggregate results.

## 8 CV comparison and automated processing

The Careers+ job-browser comparison uses the CV text you supply and named job skills or requirements to calculate overlap. With your confirmation, the text is sent to Westmere's server for the comparison. This comparison does not call OpenAI and does not itself save that CV to your profile. The result can filter or order the roles shown to you and show requirements found or not found in the supplied text. You can turn the comparison off and use ordinary search.

Application Studio separately offers AI writing assistance and role-specific analysis as described in section 9. Automated subscription and allowance checks use account identifiers, subscription status, billing periods and usage records to decide whether paid tools are available and whether a request can proceed. You can contact us if you believe these checks or a comparison result are wrong.

These tools assist preparation and access to the service. The current platform does not use an automated score to submit, reject or shortlist candidates on an employer's behalf. A skill percentage is not a hiring probability or a complete assessment of ability. Hiring teams make their own recruitment decisions. If we introduce automated processing that makes or substantially and directly contributes to decisions significantly affecting people's rights or interests, we will update the disclosures and implement the applicable safeguards before use.

## 9 Optional OpenAI assistance

When you select an available AI task, confirm the AI disclosure and request a review, we send OpenAI the supplied CV text, task and any goals and role title or description relevant to that task. Names or contact details embedded in that text are included unless you remove them. We do not separately send your account email or Stripe payment details as part of the AI prompt. Our server and hosting provider process the request on its way to and from OpenAI.

OpenAI returns suggestions such as an edited CV, cover letter or identified skills. Results are shown in your page for review and editing. The current Studio endpoint does not save prompts or generated documents in the Westmere database; profile saving and application submission are separate actions. Copying text or downloading a .txt result creates a copy under your control on your device or clipboard.

We set the OpenAI request's storage option to false. This is not Zero Data Retention: OpenAI's standard API arrangements can retain abuse-monitoring content for up to 30 days, model-related caching can retain data for up to 24 hours, and legal or safety exceptions may apply. OpenAI states that API content is not used for model training by default. We do not opt your CV content into training. See [OpenAI's API data controls](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/your-data).

OpenAI processing can occur outside Australia. Declining AI assistance does not prevent ordinary browsing or direct applications. You can stop future AI processing by not making further requests; closing the page or withdrawing consent after dispatch cannot necessarily stop an already submitted request or remove provider records immediately. Contact us about an outstanding privacy concern. Review generated material before using it and correct inaccurate or unsupported claims.

## 10 Emails marketing and other communications

### Service communications

We use Supabase Auth with Resend SMTP to send requested sign-in and account-confirmation links. Relevant providers process your address, message and delivery or security information. The approved sending identity is Westmere Careers <hello@westmere.com.au>. Keep sign-in links private; they contain one-time credentials.

We may also communicate directly about an enquiry, security issue, privacy request, payment or legal matter where necessary and lawful. Stripe can send its own billing communications according to its settings. Employer updates are currently recorded in the application timeline; a general application-email outbox, saved-search alerts and notification preference centre are not yet operating. We do not represent every in-app event as an email notification.

Unsubscribing from marketing does not stop a requested sign-in link or a necessary non-promotional account, billing, security or legal message. We do not use this distinction to place promotions in a message and avoid applicable marketing rules.

### Optional marketing

The current release does not operate a marketing mailing list, SMS marketing or telemarketing programme. If we introduce newsletters, promotional updates or job-alert emails, we will offer a separate, clearly explained opt-in before sending them and identify the message categories and any provider changes. We will treat job alerts as commercial messages where the Spam Act applies. Applying for a role, saving a CV, subscribing to Careers+ or agreeing to this policy is not marketing consent.

Our marketing rules are to use express opt-in consent, retain evidence of when and how it was given, identify Westmere accurately, provide valid sender contact details, and provide an easy unsubscribe in every commercial message. We do not use harvested-address lists or buy candidate lists for marketing. We will not send an unsolicited commercial message just to ask you to consent.

You can use the unsubscribe method in a message or contact our Privacy Officer to stop direct marketing. We action unsubscribe requests within five working days, without a fee, an account login or unnecessary extra information. The message's unsubscribe facility remains functional for at least 30 days after sending, and sender contact information remains valid for that period. A minimal suppression record may remain so we can honour your choice. Where applicable, you can also ask for the source of information used for direct marketing or ask us to stop facilitating another organisation's marketing.

### Delivery records and tracking

Email infrastructure may record sending, delivery, bounce, complaint and security events. These events do not necessarily show that a person read a message. Authentication links must not be modified by marketing click tracking. We do not currently implement marketing open pixels or behavioural email tracking. If optional tracking is introduced, we will explain what it collects and offer any legally required choice before enabling it. Providers may also perform automated spam, phishing and security analysis, including through their security subprocessors.

## 11 Service providers and other recipients

We disclose relevant information only to recipients needed for a described purpose or otherwise permitted by law. Our principal service providers are:

- **Vercel:** website hosting, server execution, content delivery and infrastructure security. Requests for account, CV, application and preparation features pass through this hosting infrastructure.
- **Supabase:** account authentication, private profiles and CV text, applications, activity events, organisation permissions, experience reports, subscription references and allowance records.
- **Resend:** sending authentication emails through Supabase SMTP and processing associated message, address, delivery and security information. Resend is not currently used by Westmere to operate a marketing list or automatically email every application event.
- **Stripe:** hosted Checkout and Customer Portal, payments, subscription administration, fraud prevention and financial compliance. We send your email and Westmere account reference to establish the customer relationship. Westmere does not collect or store full card numbers or card security codes in its application database. - **OpenAI:** the optional, expressly requested preparation assistance described in section 9.

These providers may use affiliates and subprocessors to operate their services. A supplier's list can include optional products that Westmere does not use; appearing on a supplier list does not mean every supplier receives every CV. Providers' own legal obligations can also affect their processing.

Other recipients can include the hiring organisation you apply to and its authorised team; our authorised personnel or contractors who need information for operations or support; professional advisers assisting with a specific legal, accounting or security matter; and regulators, courts, law-enforcement bodies or other persons where disclosure is required or lawfully authorised. We assess requests and limit disclosure to what is appropriate for the lawful purpose.

If a business transfer or restructure is proposed, we assess the lawful basis and safeguards before sharing personal information, minimise due-diligence information and notify affected users where required. A transaction is not an unrestricted permission to sell or repurpose CVs.

## 13 Cookies local storage and website technology

The current website uses first-party browser storage for the following functions:

- **Appearance:** `westmere-theme` in localStorage stores your Auto, Light or Dark preference.
- **Saved roles:** `westmere-saved` in localStorage stores saved job identifiers on that device. Loading the saved-role page sends the identifiers needed to retrieve the role details to our server. The current app does not sync this list into your account database.
- **Sign-in:** Supabase session cookies, including cookies whose names begin with `sb-` and relate to authentication, support signed-in requests and session refresh. Cookie expiry follows the configured authentication settings and may be refreshed while a session is active.

Appearance and saved-role storage remain until you change or clear them or your browser removes them; they are not automatically removed by signing out. You can manage or remove cookies and site storage in your browser. Blocking authentication cookies may prevent signed-in features from working. Clearing device storage does not delete a saved profile, submitted application or provider record.

We do not currently add advertising pixels, third-party behavioural analytics, session-replay tools or social-media tracking to the app. Fonts and illustrations are served locally. Hosting security and requested third-party services can still process connection information. Stripe's hosted pages and external employer websites have their own cookies and privacy arrangements. We review any proposed non-essential tracking before introducing it and obtain consent where required.

## 14 Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational steps to protect personal information against misuse, interference, loss and unauthorised access, modification or disclosure. Controls include encrypted HTTPS connections, provider-managed database encryption, authenticated and permission-scoped access, database row-level security, server-side protection of secret credentials, input validation, verified payment webhooks and controls against inappropriate caching of private responses.

Private profiles are restricted to the relevant account; application access is restricted to the candidate and authorised members of the hiring organisation, with necessary operational access for authorised service administration. Administrative credentials can permit wider access for legitimate operations and must be protected accordingly. We limit personal information in diagnostics and do not intentionally log CV text, one-time sign-in credentials or full payment details.

No service can guarantee complete security. Keep your email account and device secure, do not forward sign-in links and sign out on shared devices. Contact our Privacy Officer promptly about suspected unauthorised access. This policy does not claim that Westmere itself holds a provider's security certification or that information is end-to-end encrypted against necessary service-provider processing.

## 15 Retention deletion and account closure

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably needed for an identified lawful purpose, taking account of the service requested, whether an application or dispute remains active, security needs, access and correction obligations, and applicable financial or other recordkeeping laws. There is no universal requirement to keep every candidate CV for a fixed number of years.

Different records have different lifecycles. A profile is distinct from an application snapshot; billing records are distinct from a CV; an AI allowance record is distinct from a prompt. We assess each category separately. When information is no longer needed and no legal retention obligation applies, we take reasonable steps to securely destroy or effectively de-identify it. De-identified information is retained only where identification is no longer reasonably possible in the circumstances.

Our current app has no self-service account export or deletion button. You can request access, a copy, closure or deletion through our Privacy Officer. We will verify the request proportionately, explain what can be removed and any information that must remain, and arrange appropriate action. We do not describe a lack of a self-service button as a limit on your statutory rights.

Limited information may need to remain for a legal hold, complaint, fraud investigation, payment dispute, financial recordkeeping or to honour a marketing suppression. We restrict retained information to the relevant purpose. We do not retain an entire CV merely because a separate financial record must be kept.

Removal from active systems does not necessarily remove copies from protected backups immediately. Backup copies expire or are removed under the relevant configured provider lifecycle and are not used for ordinary operations. Where a backup is restored, necessary deletion or restriction instructions must be reapplied. Provider records subject to independent legal duties may remain for their required periods. Contact us for the applicable lifecycle for your request.

Closing a Westmere account, withdrawing an application, unsubscribing from marketing and cancelling a Stripe subscription are distinct actions. Tell us if you also need help cancelling a subscription; an unresolved payment obligation is not a general reason to retain unrelated CV information. We cannot erase information independently retained by an employer, email recipient or device owner, but will assist with requests to recipients where required by law.

## 16 Access correction and other requests

You can view or edit available profile details in your account. You may also ask our Privacy Officer for access to personal information we hold about you, a copy in a practical format, correction or annotation of inaccurate information, or consideration of a deletion or restriction request. An Australian privacy access right is not dependent on having a paid subscription. We will assist with accessible request methods where reasonably practicable.

We seek only enough information to verify identity or the authority of a representative. Please start with your account email and a description of the request; do not email identity documents unless we arrange a necessary and secure method. You do not need to use a particular form or explain why you want access. We consider information held on our behalf by providers as part of our response rather than automatically referring every request to them.

We respond within a reasonable period and aim to respond to ordinary access and correction requests within 30 days. Applicable statutory time limits take priority. Where the Victorian private-sector health-record access regime applies, access requests must be answered without unreasonable delay and within 45 days; correction requests within 30 days. If we anticipate a delay, we explain it and the next steps without extending a statutory deadline by notice alone.

We do not charge to make a request or to correct information. If a lawful and reasonable access charge is proposed, we explain it in advance and apply any relevant statutory limits. If access or correction is refused in whole or part, we provide written reasons where lawful, explain complaint options, and consider another way to provide access. You can ask us to associate a statement with information you dispute. Where required, we take reasonable steps on request to notify relevant previous recipients of a correction.

We assess additional rights under other laws where they apply. This policy does not promise that overseas privacy regimes apply merely because a provider operates in that jurisdiction, or that every record can be deleted immediately on demand.

## 17 Young people

Jobseekers can include young people. Where consent is needed, we consider whether a young person can understand the proposed handling and make a meaningful choice; where necessary, consent or assistance from a parent, guardian or other lawful representative is required. We limit information to what is necessary and do not treat parental involvement as unrestricted access to a young person's account.

We do not collect identity documents or dates of birth as a standard sign-in requirement, and we do not claim that the current sign-in flow verifies age. If you are concerned that a young person has supplied information without an appropriate basis, contact our Privacy Officer. We will assess the situation and take appropriate protective steps. Paid and AI features must also meet any applicable age, consent and provider requirements.

## 18 Questions complaints and policy changes

Contact our Privacy Officer using section 1 and describe your concern, the relevant service or information, and the outcome you seek. Complaints are free. We assess the complaint, make appropriate enquiries and aim to provide a substantive response within 30 days. If further investigation is needed, we explain the reason and expected next step.

If you are not satisfied, or we have not responded within 30 days, you may contact the [Office of the Australian Information Commissioner](https://www.oaic.gov.au/privacy/privacy-complaints/lodge-a-privacy-complaint-with-us) about a matter within its jurisdiction. Complaints about Victorian health-information handling can be made to the [Health Complaints Commissioner](https://www.hcc.vic.gov.au/public/health-records-individuals). The [Office of the Victorian Information Commissioner](https://ovic.vic.gov.au/privacy/for-the-public/your-privacy-rights/) handles matters within the Victorian public sector privacy regime, including relevant contracted services where that regime applies. You can report suspected unlawful commercial messages to the [Australian Communications and Media Authority](https://www.acma.gov.au/dealing-with-spam). A regulator determines whether it can deal with a particular complaint.

If we suspect a data breach, we take steps to contain it, assess risk, remedy the issue and reduce harm. Where the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme applies, we take all reasonable steps to complete the assessment within 30 calendar days of the relevant suspicion arising. For an eligible breach, we notify the OAIC and affected people as soon as practicable unless a lawful exception applies. A notification explains the incident, affected information and recommended protective steps. Other applicable notification duties are addressed as required.

We update this policy when our practices, providers or legal obligations change and show the revised date. For a material change, we take reasonable steps to notify affected people before the new handling begins where required. A new policy does not retrospectively create consent for an incompatible use. A copy of this policy is available free of charge, and we will take reasonable steps to provide it in another appropriate format on request.
