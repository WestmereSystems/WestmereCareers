# Trust, saved searches and email alerts

This milestone gives candidates and employers clearer information and control. The public website remains a development preview; sample vacancies and response figures are fictional.

## Employer verification and moderation

Employers submit a registered business name, ABN, website, hiring role and independently checkable evidence of authority. An ABN is not automatic proof of ownership. An independent reviewer checks business identity and hiring authority, requests more information when needed and records a reason for each decision. Existing business ownership changes require separate review.

Listings start as drafts. Employers can edit, submit for review or close them. Published content changes require another independent review. Saving an edit as a private draft withdraws publication intent. Employers receive reviewer feedback. Reviewers cannot approve their own businesses, listings, reports or concerns, and stale decisions are rejected.

Candidates can privately report a business or listing concern. Authorised employer owners and administrators can appeal response insights. Requesters follow review feedback in their trust activity. A business can be suspended after investigation; reinstatement repeats identity/authority checks and leaves its old listings closed until reviewed again.

## Candidate-reported response insights

Only reviewed, accepted reports contribute. Use the application cohort from six calendar months ago to three calendar months ago in Australia/Melbourne, with the end date excluded. Count one latest eligible application per candidate per employer and exclude current organisation members. Silence must have been reconfirmed within 30 days. Corrections and late responses return to review.

Publish figures only with at least ten unique eligible candidates. A median also needs five responders and describes responders only. “Unresponsive” means more than half of the eligible cohort reports no meaningful reply after more than two calendar months. Automated receipts and application opens do not count as meaningful replies. Older snapshots are suppressed until refreshed. Self-selection and the limits of external, self-reported evidence are explained alongside the figures.

## Saved searches and Resend alerts

Users can keep up to twenty private searches and choose optional daily or weekly digests. Explicit consent and a verified account email are required. New, approved roles matching the saved filters are eligible; no new matches means no email. CV content and private matching consent are never saved in these filters or included in alerts.

Pausing, deleting, changing filters or unsubscribing cancels unsent work. Emails include text and HTML alternatives, saved-search controls, a visible unsubscribe link and one-click unsubscribe support. Opening a confirmation page does not unsubscribe the user; submitting it does. Users can explicitly request a preview to their own verified address, limited to three requests per 24 hours. Removing a saved search does not reset this allowance.

Delivery is distinguished from provider acceptance and does not claim reading or inbox placement. Bounces, complaints and suppressions pause further alerts. Temporary provider failures use bounded retries and duplicate protection. Uncertain older requests require investigation rather than blind resending.
