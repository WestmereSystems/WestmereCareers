## 1 Required publication decisions and controls

### Identify the applicable legal obligations

Record whether the sole trader is an APP entity, including turnover, related business activities, contracting, trading in personal information and any voluntary opt-in. A turnover below $3 million does not settle the question by itself. Do not claim that publishing an APP-style policy formally opts the business into the Privacy Act. The policy uses qualified statutory language while making direct operational commitments. [OAIC small-business guidance](https://www.oaic.gov.au/privacy/privacy-guidance-for-organisations-and-government-agencies/organisations/small-business), [Privacy Act current compilation](https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2004A03712/latest/text).

Do not rely on an employer's employee-records exemption for Westmere's handling of candidates or recruitment services. The exemption does not generally protect recruitment contractors and does not cover unsuccessful job applicants in the same way as existing employee records. [OAIC employee-records guidance](https://www.oaic.gov.au/privacy/privacy-guidance-for-organisations-and-government-agencies/organisations/employee-records-exemption).

Victoria's Health Records Act can apply to health information held by ordinary businesses, including health or disability details embedded in a CV. It is not limited to health clinics. Check collection, access, correction and transfers outside Victoria, including interstate transfers. Do not impose health-service-provider minimum retention periods on all recruitment records without establishing that the provision applies. [Victorian Department of Health](https://www.health.vic.gov.au/legislation/health-records-act), [current Health Records Act](https://www.legislation.vic.gov.au/in-force/acts/health-records-act-2001).

The Victorian Privacy and Data Protection Act is principally a public-sector regime. A Victorian address alone does not bring every private business within its IPPs or protective data security reporting duties. Review any government contract and applicable outsourcing requirements before undertaking government recruitment work. [OVIC explanation of jurisdiction](https://ovic.vic.gov.au/privacy/for-the-public/your-privacy-rights/), [Victorian privacy guide](https://www.vic.gov.au/protect-privacy), [current PDP Act](https://www.legislation.vic.gov.au/in-force/acts/privacy-and-data-protection-act-2014).

The statutory tort for serious invasions of privacy commenced on 10 June 2025 and can extend beyond APP entities. A small-business exemption is not a general immunity from privacy liability. The public policy preserves legal rights instead of purporting to exclude them. [OAIC statutory-tort guidance](https://www.oaic.gov.au/privacy/your-privacy-rights/more-privacy-rights/statutory-tort-for-serious-invasions-of-privacy).

Complete the marked country disclosure in policy section 12 before publication. List countries for likely overseas recipients where practicable. Distinguish a corporate headquarters from a data-centre location. Do not list every optional vendor product as though it receives candidate information. [OAIC APP 1 country disclosures](https://www.oaic.gov.au/privacy/australian-privacy-principles/australian-privacy-principles-guidelines/chapter-1-app-1-open-and-transparent-management-of-personal-information).

Seek those country details from Supabase and document the answer. Confirm the current contractual party because marketplace contracting can differ from direct contracting. [Supabase residency FAQ](https://supabase.com/legal/privacy-resources/data-residency-and-transfers-faq), [Supabase subprocessor register](https://supabase.com/legal/customer-resources/subprocessor-list).

Vercel's Tokyo compute setting does not confine its network, logs, support or every subprocessor to Japan. Review the current customer DPA and available Trust Center list for the services actually enabled. That dynamically rendered list could not be fully retrieved during this review. [Vercel DPA](https://vercel.com/legal/dpa), [Vercel subprocessors](https://vercel.com/legal/sub-processors).

Resend states that Tokyo is a sending location, while message records are stored in the United States. Check the subscribed plan and any custom retention. Its security processing can include sampled email analysis by TypeSafe; ordinary sending is not automatically use of the optional Anthropic dashboard features. Keep authentication email content minimal. [Resend data handling](https://resend.com/security/gdpr), [Resend subprocessor list](https://resend.com/legal/subprocessors).

Confirm OpenAI's project residency, relevant model behaviour, data-sharing controls and contractual terms. The app uses the standard client without a regional endpoint override; this does not establish a regional residency commitment. Keep optional training/data sharing off for candidate content and record evidence of the settings. The policy already distinguishes disabled response storage from abuse monitoring and caching. [Official OpenAI data controls](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/your-data), [OpenAI residency scope](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/data-residency).

Review Stripe's current contracting and payment-processing arrangements for Australian customers. Its public policy announces an update on 20 November 2026, so recheck that version if publication occurs on or after that date. Stripe's independent retention and financial obligations should not be portrayed as controlled entirely by Westmere. [Stripe privacy policy](https://stripe.com/au/privacy).

Record APP 8 reasonable steps and any applicable Victorian health-information transfer basis. EU standard contractual clauses, a provider's GDPR claim or its SOC certification do not automatically establish Australian compliance. Avoid a general consent waiver of APP 8 accountability. [OAIC APP 8 guidance](https://www.oaic.gov.au/privacy/australian-privacy-principles/australian-privacy-principles-guidelines/chapter-8-app-8-cross-border-disclosure-of-personal-information).

### Make privacy requests work

Monitor support@westmere.com.au and assign a responsible person with a backup. Test receipt of a privacy request. Establish a secure manual access, correction, export and deletion process before accepting live candidates. A self-service button is not itself a statutory requirement, but the repository explicitly records the full workflow as unfinished.

Cover Auth users and sessions, profiles, submitted snapshots, events, reports, memberships, billing references, usage records, relevant provider records and backups. Verify identity proportionately and export only the requesting person's information. Revoke relevant sessions as part of account closure; deleting an Auth user alone does not invalidate all existing access tokens. Coordinate subscription cancellation without making access or correction contingent on payment.

Submitted application snapshots are protected against ordinary updates. Design an authorised correction or annotation path, with an audit record and any required notification to previous recipients. Do not tell users that an immutable database design defeats their correction rights. [OAIC APP 12 access](https://www.oaic.gov.au/privacy/australian-privacy-principles/australian-privacy-principles-guidelines/chapter-12-app-12-access-to-personal-information), [OAIC APP 13 correction](https://www.oaic.gov.au/privacy/australian-privacy-principles/australian-privacy-principles-guidelines/chapter-13-app-13-correction-of-personal-information).

The public policy's 30-day ordinary response target is a proposed business commitment. Do not describe that as a universal statutory deadline for private organisations under APP 12. Applicable Victorian private-sector health-record limits are 45 days for access and 30 days for correction, without unreasonable delay. Set internal deadlines earlier than those limits. [Health Complaints Commissioner access guidance](https://www.hcc.vic.gov.au/public/health-records-individuals).

### Implement the retention programme

The database does not currently enforce a full category-based retention schedule. Approve and implement one, including provider logs, backups, legal holds and deletion after restoration. The proposal in section 3 is a starting point for business/legal approval, not an existing control or a statement of universal legal minimums. Retention needs and data minimisation must be assessed together. [OAIC APP 11 security and disposal](https://www.oaic.gov.au/privacy/australian-privacy-principles/australian-privacy-principles-guidelines/chapter-11-app-11-security-of-personal-information).

### Complete collection notices and specific consent

Show short, prominent collection notices at sign-in, profile saving, application submission, experience reporting, AI requests and any future marketing opt-in. Link the full policy and use the actual operator and contact. A footer link alone should not be assumed sufficient for every collection. [OAIC APP 5 notices](https://www.oaic.gov.au/privacy/australian-privacy-principles/australian-privacy-principles-guidelines/chapter-5-app-5-notification-of-the-collection-of-personal-information).

Current application and AI routes require a consent boolean but do not persist a full consent record. Store purpose, notice/policy version, time, user/request reference and the choice without logging the CV. An application-sharing checkbox is not automatically specific, informed consent to collect health or other sensitive information. Either prevent unnecessary sensitive submissions and explain this prominently, or add the particular lawful consent/handling process required before collection. A general policy cannot fix this by retrospectively asserting consent. [OAIC APP 3 collection](https://www.oaic.gov.au/privacy/australian-privacy-principles/australian-privacy-principles-guidelines/chapter-3-app-3-collection-of-solicited-personal-information).

### Keep marketing disabled until its controls exist

The app has no operational marketing list, outbox or preference centre. The policy deliberately says so. Its future-marketing terms are commitments to implement before activation. Adopt separate express consent, evidence of consent, suppression handling and a tested unsubscribe route for every campaign or commercial job alert. Respect an unsubscribe received by support as well as the embedded link. Do not require sign-in or extra identifying information to unsubscribe. Treat mixed transactional/promotional messages according to their content. [ACMA spam rules](https://www.acma.gov.au/avoid-sending-spam), [ACMA unsubscribe requirements](https://www.acma.gov.au/sites/default/files/2024-05/Fact%20sheet%20-%20email%20and%20SMS%20unsubscribe%20rules.pdf).

The policy chooses express consent as Westmere's standard. That is a business safeguard; the Spam Act can recognise inferred consent in limited circumstances. The Act applies regardless of small-business turnover. The policy does not authorise SMS, calls, prospect-list uploads or bought lists. Review any new channel, including Do Not Call obligations and applicable sender requirements, before introducing it. [ACMA consumer-consent expectations](https://www.acma.gov.au/articles/2024-06/consent-expectations-businesses-using-direct-marketing), [OAIC APP 7 direct marketing](https://www.oaic.gov.au/privacy/australian-privacy-principles/australian-privacy-principles-guidelines/chapter-7-app-7-direct-marketing).

### Verify security and incident operations

The source implements permissions, RLS, request validation, private caching rules and payment verification, but this review is not a penetration test. Complete the existing hosted isolation, withdrawal, sign-in, billing and synthetic AI checks in the release checklist. Confirm provider encryption and backups for the selected plan, administrative MFA, least privilege, access revocation, operational logs with redaction, restore/deletion drills and an incident contact roster. Do not add a claim of end-to-end encryption, 24-hour monitoring or an independent Westmere certification without evidence.

Create a breach response procedure with containment, evidence preservation, risk assessment, supplier coordination and notifications. The NDB assessment period is not permission to wait 30 days before an otherwise required notification: eligible-breach notifications are due as soon as practicable once the obligation arises. [OAIC breach response guide](https://www.oaic.gov.au/privacy/notifiable-data-breaches/quick-reference-guide-for-responding-to-data-breaches).

### Review the December 2026 changes and young users

APP 1.7–1.9 automated-decision transparency obligations commence on **10 December 2026**. The updated policy explains CV inputs, filtering/ranking, AI assistance, entitlement/usage checks and their effects without asserting that all are necessarily significant decisions. Obtain an applicability assessment; a human decision later in a process does not automatically remove the obligation. [OAIC updated APP 1 guidance](https://www.oaic.gov.au/privacy/australian-privacy-principles/australian-privacy-principles-guidelines/chapter-1-app-1-open-and-transparent-management-of-personal-information).

Decide the intended user ages and how lawful consent and provider restrictions will work for young jobseekers. The app currently has no age verification or guardian flow; the policy does not invent one. Review applicability, final text and actual commencement/transitional arrangements of the Children's Online Privacy Code. The OAIC is required to register it by 10 December 2026; a registration deadline should not be presented as proof that every provision has already commenced. A careers website may be used by under-18s even without marketing specifically to children. [OAIC Children's Online Privacy Code](https://www.oaic.gov.au/privacy/privacy-registers/privacy-codes/childrens-online-privacy-code).

## 3 Proposed retention schedule

Approve each period against the actual purpose, expected candidate experience, supplier plans and applicable law. Implement earlier deletion where information is unnecessary. Legal holds must identify a basis, a limited dataset, an owner and a review/expiry trigger; they should not freeze every record indefinitely.

| Record category | Proposed operational rule | Decision or exception |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Unsaved CV/editor/Studio text | Page memory only; no application-level persistence | Provider safety/caching records and browser/device copies have separate lifecycles |
| Private profile and CV | While actively needed for the account; review after 24 months of inactivity, notify before removal | Earlier valid deletion request; confirm what counts as inactivity |
| Application snapshots and timeline | Review for deletion 12 months after role closure or withdrawal | Preserve longer only for an identified active purpose or legal duty; give candidates a way to retain their copy |
| Identifiable experience reports | Review at 12 months after last confirmation or resolution | Public release is a separate future approval; do not retain identifiers merely to support an anonymous statistic |
| AI allowance metadata | Billing period plus 12 months for disputes/audit | No prompts or generated documents in the allowance table |
| Routine request and security logs | Prefer 30 days; document a demonstrated need before extending up to 90 days | Incident evidence only for the incident/claim lifecycle; provider plans must be checked |
| Routine closed support correspondence | Review 24 months after closure | Minimise attachments earlier; special complaints/legal holds separately justified |
| Financial records | Apply the actual tax-law period and relevant claim/transaction requirements | Confirm with the sole trader's accountant; do not assume company-specific seven-year requirements apply to this operator |
| Future marketing consent evidence | While relied upon, then for a documented complaint/enforcement need | Keep only the evidence required; approve an actual post-withdrawal period |
| Future marketing suppression | Minimal email or suitable matching identifier while needed to prevent re-enrolment | Restricted use; removing the whole account must not silently re-subscribe someone |
| Device-local role IDs and appearance | Until changed, cleared or removed by the browser | Signing out does not currently clear them |
| Backups and provider records | Document plan-specific expiry and deletion on restored data | No invented universal 30- or 90-day guarantee; record each supplier's actual configuration |

These periods are recommendations, not legally prescribed recruitment retention periods and not evidence of deployed deletion jobs. The policy intentionally uses purpose-based retention criteria until a schedule has been approved and implemented.

Our Privacy Policy explains access, correction, complaints and overseas handling, including Japan, the United States, Singapore and any additional countries confirmed in the final policy.

### Sign in

We use your email address to create or access your account and send your requested sign-in link through Supabase and Resend. Without it, we cannot provide an account. Email records may be stored in the United States; authentication and other provider processing can occur overseas as explained in our Privacy Policy. Signing in does not subscribe you to marketing.

### Save a private profile

Saving stores this name and CV text in our private Supabase database in Japan, with associated overseas provider processing described in our Privacy Policy. Employers cannot browse your private profile. An application creates a separate shared copy. You may leave out unnecessary details and continue browsing without saving a CV. Do not include government identifiers or unnecessary sensitive information.

### Submit an application

Submitting shares the CV, cover letter and answers shown here with [DISPLAY THE ACTUAL ADVERTISING ORGANISATION] and its authorised hiring team, and stores a separate application snapshot and activity history. The application cannot be submitted without its required material. Editing your profile will not change this copy. Withdrawal stops further workspace access but does not recall copies already received. See our Privacy Policy for overseas processing and correction/deletion requests, and the advertiser's notice for its own handling.

**Separate required choice:** I want to submit this application and share the material shown with [DISPLAY THE ACTUAL ADVERTISING ORGANISATION].

### Compare a CV in the job browser

This optional comparison sends the CV text to Westmere's server to compare named skills with job requirements. It can filter or order your results; it does not call OpenAI or save the CV to your profile. Missing text evidence is not proof of missing ability. Ordinary search remains available without this comparison. Server processing occurs overseas as described in our Privacy Policy.

### Request OpenAI assistance

If you request this review, the supplied CV, selected task and relevant goals/role information will be sent to OpenAI through our hosting infrastructure. Remove unnecessary sensitive information and other people's details first. Processing may occur overseas; disabling response storage does not mean zero provider retention. The Privacy Policy explains the provider's handling and your choices. Declining does not prevent ordinary browsing or applying.

**Separate required choice:** I agree to send the text shown to OpenAI for this requested review. I have read the AI and overseas-processing information and will check the suggestions before using them.

This choice is not a waiver of APP 8 obligations and does not replace any specific consent needed to collect sensitive information.

### Report an application experience

We record your account/application reference, employer, dates and response information privately to review your report. It is not automatically published, and a live public aggregation service is not currently operating. Reporting is optional. See our Privacy Policy for access, correction, retention and overseas handling.

### Future optional marketing

**Optional unticked choice:** Email me [SPECIFY NEWSLETTER OR JOB-ALERT CATEGORY] from Westmere Careers. This is optional. I can unsubscribe at any time using the email link or support@westmere.com.au. See the Privacy Policy for how my email and preferences are handled overseas.
