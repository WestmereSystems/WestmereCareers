# Search directories

Westmere bundles a snapshot of real reference data, so suggestions do not send search text to an external autocomplete provider. Existing curated suggestions remain first in the directory.

- **Places:** GeoNames Australian gazetteer, retrieved 17 November 2026. 18,622 populated places and localities, including suburbs, with state and coordinates. Historic, abandoned and destroyed places are excluded. Attribution: [GeoNames](https://www.geonames.org/), [source](https://download.geonames.org/export/dump/AU.zip), [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Derived snapshot: `src/content/australian-locations.json` (name, state, latitude, longitude). Duplicates with the same name/state use the largest recorded population. This is not an address directory.
- **Job titles:** Australian Bureau of Statistics, OSCA 2024 version 1.0, index of principal titles, alternative titles and specialisations, retrieved 17 November 2026. [Source workbook](https://www.abs.gov.au/statistics/classifications/osca-occupation-standard-classification-australia/2024-version-1-0/data-downloads/OSCA%20index%20of%20principal%20titles%20alternative%20titles%20and%20specialisations.xlsx). Attribution: © Commonwealth of Australia (Australian Bureau of Statistics), [CC BY 4.0](https://www.abs.gov.au/website-privacy-copyright-and-disclaimer/copyright). Derived snapshot: `src/content/occupation-titles.json`. Occupation classification labels are suggestions, not a requirement to use an official title.

Distances are straight-line kilometres between locality centres, not driving distances. A state is required for ambiguous locality names. Nationwide remote roles remain eligible for every radius. Listings without resolvable coordinates are excluded from radius searches; free-text location search remains available.

## Discovery region outlines

The interactive state/territory map derives its eight outlines and two Indian Ocean Territory insets from the [ABS ASGS 2021 generalised state/territory layer](https://geo.abs.gov.au/arcgis/rest/services/ASGS2021/STE/FeatureServer/1). The [ABS digital-boundary documentation](https://www.abs.gov.au/statistics/standards/australian-statistical-geography-standard-asgs/edition-3-july-2021-june-2026/access-and-downloads/digital-boundary-files) identifies the custodian and boundary data. Attribution: © Commonwealth of Australia, Australian Bureau of Statistics, ASGS Edition 3 (2021), CC BY 4.0; modified for display. The bundled SVG paths are simplified and projected for an overview, not survey or legal-boundary use. Capital markers use the existing GeoNames city directory and the same projection as the outlines. Paths are bundled locally; displaying the map does not contact a tile or geolocation service.

Christmas Island and Cocos (Keeling) Islands are Australian external territories. They are grouped with WA for Westmere reporting by explicit product choice; this does not label them as legally part of that state. This grouping is consistent with their [WA service-delivery arrangements](https://www.infrastructure.gov.au/territories-regions/territories/indian-ocean-territories-christmas-island-and-cocos-keeling-islands). Trusted platform GeoIP country codes CX and CC map to the WA aggregate, while the display uses labelled island insets. Exact visitor coordinates are not stored.
