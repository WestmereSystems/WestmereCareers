# Agency client advertising declarations

Verified agency owners and entitled administrators add a private client in Profile → Clients, then record a current advertising declaration. A client can have a Westmere employer account or no account. Neither routine employer confirmation nor routine Admin review gates ordinary client advertising. Business verification, agency publication eligibility, paid plan capacity, recruiter assignment, vacancy moderation and closed-marketplace restrictions still apply.

The declaration records its actor, time, version (`agency-advertising-v1`), external agreement reference and scope, dates (maximum one year), and permission for disclosed client names/branding. The agency retains the agreement and must provide evidence on request. Each client vacancy requires confirmation of authority and a genuine current opening. Agency-declared records are labelled as such; they never imply employer verification. The agency remains the advertiser and initial applicant-data recipient. Confidential client identities stay private. Linking a client grants no employer account, profile-management or shared candidate access. Agencies and businesses cannot apply for jobs on behalf of anyone.

Employer owners see Agency relationships, optional confirmation, separately requested offer permission and a dispute action. A dispute pauses associated advertising, applicant intake, candidate access and unsent recruitment communications, records a hold and appears at the top of Admin's paginated agency client register. Unregistered employers can use the existing listing report process or hello@westmere.com.au. No unsolicited verification email or automatic employer account is created.

Admin can audit any relationship (including an ordinary declared relationship), request evidence and pause advertising, review evidence, reject/revoke a relationship or clear a resolved historical hold. Administrative decisions require an independent reviewer, a reason and a current record version. Agencies can submit evidence but cannot clear holds. Duplicate client records matching the employer, client name or representative email cannot bypass a hold. Restoring authority does not automatically reopen closed ads. Audit events preserve declarations, optional confirmations, disputes, evidence responses and review decisions; the Admin activity feed also records changes.

Offer communication remains separate: the agency or employer requests it, a linked employer must explicitly confirm it, and an independent reviewer must check the offer authority. For clients without accounts the reviewer verifies explicit external authority. Ordinary declarations never enable offer communication. Withdrawing offer authority also cancels queued decision emails under that relationship. No binding employment-contract execution or candidate proxy applications are introduced.

Approved historical mandates remain intact. Pending requests are not silently converted into consent; the agency must accept the new declaration. No historical test client or vacancy is released by this migration. SQL changes run atomically and add only private records behind authenticated RPCs, with RLS and direct table access revoked.
