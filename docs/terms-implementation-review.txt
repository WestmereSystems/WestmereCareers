CURRENT IMPLEMENTATION GAP — AFFIRMATIVE TERMS ACCEPTANCE

The existing email-code and OAuth flows authenticate an account but do not contain
an affirmative Terms acceptance control or a durable, versioned acceptance
record. The document does not treat entering a code as acceptance, or claim that
an email OTP is a separate two-factor authentication feature. This document task
does not implement a new authentication or acceptance system.

Before relying on the Terms as an account contract, add an accessible, unticked
agreement checkbox with links to the Terms and Privacy Policy. Apply it to every
account-creation/completion path, including an account implicitly created through
an email code, Google, Microsoft or another enabled method. A shared completion
step after verified authentication can cover all methods consistently. Existing
accounts need an appropriate notice and acceptance process; do not backdate it.

Suggested account agreement text

"I agree to the Westmere Careers Terms and Conditions and confirm that I am at
least 16 years old, with parent or guardian permission where required."

Separate accompanying notice

"The Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information. Agreeing to the
Terms does not subscribe you to marketing or authorise optional AI processing."

Suggested completion button

"Agree and continue"

Record acceptance only after an authenticated person's deliberate action, tied
to the account, exact Terms version/content hash, server timestamp and completion
method. Validate the version on the server, protect records against ordinary
client alteration and retain the exact accepted wording. Do not depend on an
editable user-metadata flag or a browser checkbox as the only audit record. Use
proportionate retention; there is no need to collect identity documents or raw
IP addresses just to record agreement. Update the Privacy Policy if acceptance
records introduce a new information-handling practice.

Declining new Terms must still allow access to the legal pages, support, privacy
rights, cancellation of an existing paid renewal and account-closure assistance.
Do not automatically accept on behalf of a user, conflate accepting Terms with
marketing/AI consent, or regard a successful OAuth callback as agreement.

BILLING AND OPERATIONAL ALIGNMENT

Show the renewal total, period, shared allowance, cancellation method and links
to the current Terms immediately before a paid purchase. Record the accepted
version with the purchase or order. Confirm that Stripe's live portal actually
permits online cancellation and invoice access; the existing release notes still
list end-to-end live-billing validation as a launch gate. These Terms do not
enable live charging or amend existing Stripe prices.

The code currently validates a specific A$25 Stripe price and exact-price
entitlement. A future price increase needs an intentional product/billing
migration that preserves existing paid access, the required notice and an
opportunity to cancel. Editing this document or replacing an environment price
identifier alone is not a compliant subscription migration.

Implement the promised notice, cancellation/refund support, moderation and appeal
procedures in daily operations. Staff must distinguish an application open, a
meaningful response, delivery and reading. The Terms cannot make incomplete
employer activity complete or certify external application delivery.

The 16+ service rule does not itself verify age. Add an appropriate attestation
and a lawful handling route for underage accounts. Do not collect full dates of
birth or identity documents merely because they are technically available.

Future employer/agency orders must state quantities, duration, seats/client use,
tax, total price, any minimum term, renewal, credit expiry and cancellation. The
current Terms deliberately do not invent fees, automatic annual commitments,
placement commissions, unlimited seats or undisclosed sponsored ranking.

Keep a dated archive of each published version and use direct notices when the
material-change clause requires them. A publication date alone does not show
that an existing user accepted a variation. Provider changes, new advertising
products or materially different AI/hiring automation require a fresh review.

LEGAL REVIEW

This is complete public-facing drafting based on the inspected implementation
and operator-confirmed choices. It is not a solicitor's opinion, a guarantee of
enforceability or certification that the service is ready for a paid public
launch. Obtain Australian legal review of the contract, assent flow and actual
business operations before relying on it in production. In particular, review
minor users, consumer/small-business protections, agency/licensing activities,
report publication/defamation risk, data handling and any negotiated business
liability or payment provisions. Do not replace that review with a general
"use at your own risk" or "no refunds" clause.

The Terms preserve non-excludable rights, permit fair correction and appeal,
avoid an unlimited consumer indemnity or blanket negligence exclusion, and do
not impose compulsory arbitration, exclusive Victorian consumer venue, shortened
statutory claim periods or an entire-agreement exclusion of actionable promises.
