# Westmere Careers visual refresh

Implements the approved forest-and-sage concept in the existing Next.js application. Current homepage copy, search, employer review, applications, private CVs, billing, trust workflows, system/light appearance and accessibility controls remain available.

## Connected features

- Homepage roles come from `getPublicJobs({ sort: "newest", pageSize: 12 })`, the same published/approved/non-expired inventory used by search. The carousel refreshes `/api/jobs?sort=newest` every minute while visible, and on returning to the page. It retains the sample-data notice in demo environments and never fills a live error with fictional vacancies. Manual controls, touch scrolling, pause on hover/focus and reduced-motion preferences are supported.
- `/account` holds Profile, Applications, Saved roles, Searches & alerts, Notifications, Trust activity and Settings as horizontal links with active underlines. The previous routes redirect to the corresponding tab; saved-search query parameters are retained. Document history, account email changes, communication preferences, billing and sign-out remain available.
- The notification bell previews the four latest updates, shows an unread count and links to the full profile tab. Loading a preview does not mark notifications read. The full tab provides explicit read controls and older-notification pagination.
- Employer statistics use an authenticated route restricted to active, verified hiring memberships. Only submission dates, current stages and event timestamps are selected. The dashboard never fetches CVs, identities, cover letters, answers or message content and never records a first view. Date/business/role filters, previous-period comparisons, trends, current stages, review/update timing, role comparisons and CSV export use real authorised records. Withdrawals are excluded consistently with existing access rules. External applications, cumulative conversion and successful hires are not inferred.
- The sidebar and full Studio share the existing validated OpenAI review contract. A signed browser cookie permits one guest review; a verified account has three additional reviews. Paid users continue through the existing 30-request billing-period reservation flow. Trial reservations are atomic, server-only and separate from paid usage, with failed-request refunds, expiry, five requests per daily keyed network identifier and a global 100-trial-attempt daily ceiling. The ledger stores no CVs, outputs or raw IPs. Browsers cannot grant themselves credits by changing local state. The cookie lasts one year; account usage cascades on account deletion. Guest records expire after a year and failed attempts after 30 days, cleaned on subsequent reservations.

The trial migration is additive and does not enable credentials, billing, live candidate collection or public job posting in a demo environment. Public demonstration and connected staging retain their existing environment configuration.

## Brand and imagery

The site and email wordmarks share the lowercase **w**, asymmetric sage badge and spaced **CAREERS** lettering. Email clients use the checked-in PNG, including a dark background so the wordmark is legible in existing light templates. Regenerate it with `node scripts/generate-brand-assets.mjs`. Template URLs include a version query to refresh mail image caches. Hosted Auth templates and the separate email worker require their normal release process; changing source alone does not update provider settings. The existing public PNG URL also serves the new artwork after website deployment.

The footer now uses a simple path, milestone dots and leaf in `public/illustrations/career-path.svg`.

`public/images/australian-wildlife-worker.webp` is an original image generated using the built-in image-generation tool on 15 November 2026, resized to 1,600 pixels wide and encoded as WebP. It is labelled illustrative, and represents no real employee, organisation or testimonial. The approved `focused-work.webp` hero is unchanged.

The visual references were [Employment Hero Jobs](https://employmenthero.com/jobs/) and [WorkinAUS](https://workinaus.com.au/), particularly their restrained dark surfaces, large headings, generous spacing and Australian workplace imagery. Competitor photographs, claims, testimonials and branding have not been copied.

### Final image-generation prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: original editorial photograph for the lower homepage of Westmere Careers, an Australian jobs platform with a dark forest-green, sage and cream design. Primary request: depict an Australian wildlife sanctuary worker with wallabies or kangaroos, conveying meaningful work in Australia. Scene: a calm eucalyptus-lined wildlife sanctuary in regional Victoria, soft dry green grass and natural late afternoon light. Subject: one adult female wildlife carer wearing a simple sage-green work shirt and practical dark trousers, crouching at a respectful distance beside two relaxed wallabies; holding a small metal feed bucket, smiling naturally. Plausible anatomy, authentic documentary workplace atmosphere, no petting or staged tourist selfie. Composition: wide landscape photograph, worker and animals predominantly on right and centre, quieter foliage and lawn on left for a dark translucent text overlay; include full enough bodies to make wallabies recognisable. Natural colour and skin texture, premium editorial photography, moderate depth of field. No text, logos, watermarks, typography, UI, borders or collage. This is a fictional illustrative scene, not a real employee or employer testimonial.
